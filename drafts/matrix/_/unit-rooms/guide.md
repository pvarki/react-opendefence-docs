# Unit rooms & organisation

META: slides

Matrix works well as your unit's primary messaging tool. This is a recommended
way to structure your rooms — treat each room like a radio channel.

## Easy primary messaging tool

![Element Space with the unit's rooms](./01-step.png)

Matrix fulfils all messaging and video-call needs well. All members of this
server are automatically members of your server's Space, so you can start
communicating with your team immediately.

## A good room structure

![Example room structure in the Space](./02-step.png)

In addition to the automatically created rooms, we recommend creating the
following rooms and inviting the appropriate members. The core idea is to think
of rooms as radio channels.

## Command room

![Command room conversation](./03-step.png)

Invite your team leaders to the **Command** channel. It works like a radio
command channel — for orders and their acknowledgements. It can also be used for
briefings over video conference.

## Friendly situation room

![Friendly situation room conversation](./04-step.png)

Invite at least your team leaders and deputies to the **Friendly situation**
room. Use it to communicate the execution of your mission — where friendlies are
moving and how task execution is progressing.

## Recon room

![Recon room conversation](./05-step.png)

Invite at least your team leaders and deputies to the **Recon** room. Use it to
communicate reconnaissance and enemy observations.

## Guard room

![Guard room conversation](./06-step.png)

Set up the **Guard** room so that anyone can freely join from the Space.
Operators starting guard or patrol duties join for the duration of their task,
giving the command post a direct line to them.

## Other room recommendations

Other recommended rooms include a **Call for Fires** room and a **Fires
Management** room for fire direction and fire coordination.

## User management

A user set as an administrator in Deploy App automatically receives
administrator rights to the Space and its rooms, and is added to the admin room.
A user removed from Deploy App loses access to Matrix.

## Working with other Matrix servers

By default, you can invite users from other Matrix servers to your rooms. This
enables cooperation with other teams using Deploy App, or with NATO allies who
also use Matrix — for example through the NI2CE application.
