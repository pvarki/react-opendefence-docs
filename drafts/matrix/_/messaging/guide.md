# Messaging in Matrix

META: slides

Element covers all your messaging needs — text, voice messages, calls and video.
Here is what you can do once you are signed in.

## Messaging in Element

![Element conversation view with a text message](./01-step.png)

Element supports text messages, voice messages, voice and video calls, and video
conferences. These work in any room or direct message conversation.

## Space and rooms

![Element Space view listing the pre-created rooms](./02-step.png)

You automatically join this server's Space as a member. The Space includes all
server members and has pre-created rooms, so you can start communicating right
away.

## Messaging in rooms

![Sending a message in a room](./03-step.png)

Send messages in any room or start a direct message with other members. You can
also create new rooms that are either discoverable by all Space members or
join-by-invitation only.

## Voice and video calls

![Starting a call from a room](./04-step.png)

Start a voice or video call directly from any room or direct message —
convenient for quick conversations without setting up a meeting.

## Group video call

![Group video call in a room](./05-step.png)

Element supports high-quality group video calls in rooms — fully equivalent to
Microsoft Teams, Skype, or other similar services.

## Encryption

![Element encryption indicator](./06-step.png)

A key feature of Matrix is strong end-to-end encryption: outsiders — including
the server itself — cannot access your private messages or group messages.
