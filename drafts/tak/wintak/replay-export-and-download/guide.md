# Export, download & manage

META: slides

Recorded tracks can be exported, pulled from a TAK Server, and managed from the
recordings folder.

## Export tracks

![Exported marker track drawn on the map](./01-screenshot.png)

Export a recording's moved-marker tracks as **GPX**, **KML** or **KMZ** to
**Documents > WinTAK > Export**. All marker types are supported except Vehicle
Outlines and Vehicle Models. A re-imported track appears on the map, and its
marker follows the track during playback.

## Download events from a TAK Server

![Download Events panel with region, dates and server](./02-screenshot.png)

Create a recording from past server events (within a 24-hour window). Define a map
region, choose a **Start** and **End** date/time (defaults to a two-hour block
ending now), pick the **Server** if more than one is connected, then **Download**
and **OK**.

## Manage recordings & preferences

![TAK Replay Preferences — Auto Record, Pan to Map Events, directory](./03-screenshot.png)

Recordings (.cpr) are stored under **AppData > Roaming > WinTAK > Databases >
TakReplay**; use **Open Recordings Folder** to view them — you can also drop .cpr
files in directly. In **Settings / Preferences** set **Auto Record** (record on
startup), **Pan to Map Events**, and the **Recordings Directory**.
