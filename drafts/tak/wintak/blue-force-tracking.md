# Blue force tracking

META: slides

WinTAK shows you and everyone in your group on one shared map — this is blue force tracking. Here's how to read the map, see your group, and set your own position when your PC has no GPS.

## Starting position

![Starts at 0/0 with no GPS](_assets/Kuva32.png)

If your PC has no GPS, WinTAK starts at the 0/0 coordinates. You'll move the map to your area and set your position manually.

## Move and zoom the map

![Move the map with the mouse](_assets/Kuva33.png)

Move the map **with the mouse** toward Finland. Zoom with the mouse wheel, the plus/minus keys, or the on-screen buttons.

## Straighten the map

![Compass icon](_assets/Kuva34.png)

Click the **compass icon (N)** to straighten the map to north-up.

## See your group

![Group members as coloured icons](_assets/Kuva35.png)

Other users in your group appear as coloured icons. Each icon's colour is the colour that user set for themselves.

## Set your location manually

![Red information field, bottom-right](_assets/Kuva37.png)

If your device has no GPS, set your own location: press the **red information field** in the bottom-right corner.

## Pick your position

![Click your position on the map](_assets/Kuva36.png)

Find your location on the map and click it. Your own marker then appears — and becomes visible to other users.

## Change your location

![Select a new location](_assets/Kuva38.png)

To move yourself later, click your own marker (the **arrow symbol**), then click a new location.
