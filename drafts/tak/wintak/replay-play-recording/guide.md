# Play a recording

META: slides

Any saved recording can be replayed in the Timeline Viewer.

## Open & play a recording

![Timeline Viewer at the bottom of the map](./01-screenshot.png)

Select **Open** next to a filename to open the Timeline Viewer at the bottom of
the map. It shows the start/end and total time, player speed, current and elapsed
time, and a green mark for each event. Chats sent or received during the recording
appear in a panel beside the viewer. Select **Play** to start; only events inside
the recording region display, at the point in time they occurred.

## Control playback speed

![Timeline player controls](./02-screenshot.png)

Each press of fast-forward or reverse changes the speed up to **64x** or down to
**−64x**; a negative speed plays backwards toward the start. Tick **Pan to Map
Events** (off by default) to keep replayed events in view as they play.
