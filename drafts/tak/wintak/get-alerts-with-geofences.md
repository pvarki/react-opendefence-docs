# Get alerts with Geofences

META: slides

A geofence is a boundary you draw on the map that raises an alert when something crosses it — so you can watch an area without staring at the screen.

> Draft: written without screenshots. Add WinTAK geofence screenshots when available.

## What a geofence does

A geofence monitors a shape on the map and notifies you when a tracked entity **enters** or **exits** it. Use it to watch an avenue of approach, a boundary, or a no-go area.

## Create a geofence

Draw an **area** (circle or polygon) with the drawing tools, then turn it into a geofence from its options. The geofence is tied to that shape.

## Set the alert conditions

Choose what triggers the alert — entering and/or exiting — and which entities to monitor (for example hostile or unknown tracks). Tune it so you get useful alerts, not noise.

## Receive alerts

When the condition is met, WinTAK alerts you and highlights the crossing on the map. Acknowledge it, assess, and report through the RECON feed or your messaging tools as appropriate.
