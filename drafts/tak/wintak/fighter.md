# Fighter

META: slides

As a fighter, WinTAK keeps you on the shared map and lets you feed observations to the command post. Here is what you send and what you get.

## What a fighter sends

- Your **live position**, so the unit sees you on the map (blue force tracking).
- **Observation markers** — create a marker and send it to the HQ role / the observations feed.
- **Reports** over the prescribed messaging tools, on the command, recon, BLUFOR and common channels.

## What a fighter gets

- The CP-produced **RECON Feed** — what the unit knows about the area of operations.
- Mission **PLAN** feeds — marching routes, patrol routes, geofence alerts.
- Other users' positions on the map.

## How to do it

- See **Blue force tracking** to find yourself, read the map, and set your position when you have no GPS.
- Make sure you are connected first — a **green cloud** in the TAK Network Status means the server connection is up; if your RECON feed is empty, re-check the connection (see the Start guide).
