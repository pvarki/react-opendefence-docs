# Use GeoChat

META: slides

GeoChat is WinTAK's built-in text messaging. Use it to coordinate with your unit without leaving the map.

> Draft: written without screenshots. Add WinTAK GeoChat screenshots when available.

## Open GeoChat

Open **GeoChat** from the WinTAK toolbar. It lists your conversations — all-chat, teams/groups, and individual users.

## Choose who you're messaging

Pick the recipient: the whole net (**All Chat Rooms**), a specific **team / group**, or a single **user**. Sending to the right scope keeps the net clean and respects OPSEC.

## Send a message

Type your message and send. Keep it short and unambiguous; follow your unit's reporting format and use cover terms for sensitive detail.

## Messages and the map

GeoChat is geo-aware — messages can carry a location, and you can jump from a message to the point on the map it refers to. For longer or formatted reporting, use the prescribed messaging tools per your unit's standing orders.
