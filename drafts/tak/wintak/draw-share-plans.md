# Draw & share Plans

META: slides

WinTAK's drawing tools let you sketch plans — routes, areas and control measures — and share them with your unit through a feed.

> Draft: written without screenshots. Add WinTAK drawing-tool screenshots when available.

## Open the drawing tools

Open the drawing tools from the WinTAK toolbar. You can place lines, routes, shapes and freehand drawings directly on the map.

## Draw routes and areas

Draw what the plan needs — a marching or patrol **route**, an **area** (polygon/circle) for a sector or objective, or control measures. Snap points onto the map and adjust them as needed.

## Label and style

Give each drawing a clear **name** and set its colour so the plan reads at a glance and matches your unit's conventions.

## Share to a feed

Send the finished plan to a **Data Sync feed** (for example a PLAN feed) so the whole unit receives it and stays in sync as you update it.
