# Deploy App's TAK page

META: slides

## Open Deploy App's TAK page

Everything you need to set up WinTAK comes from Deploy App's **TAK** page. Open Deploy App and go to the **TAK** page.

## Get WinTAK and your Client Package

The TAK page gives WinTAK users two things:

- a link to download the **WinTAK** application from the Defence Forces' designated location, and
- the green **Download your Client Package** button — your personal `Name.zip` that connects WinTAK to your unit's server.

When you have both, start with the **Make sure you have the App** guide below.
