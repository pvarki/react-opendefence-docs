# Send an Emergency Alert Beacon

META: slides

An emergency alert beacon broadcasts your location to every TAK user on the server until you cancel it. You can set one from anywhere.

## See alerts on the map

![Map widget showing an emergency alert and its bearing from your own marker](./01-screenshot.png)

When any TAK user sets a beacon, the widget in the lower left of their map shows where the alert is in relation to their own marker.

## Start a beacon and pick a type

![Emergency Beacon menu listing 911 Alert, Geo-fence Breached, Ring the Bell and In Contact](./02-screenshot.png)

Tap **Emergency** on the TAK Tracker toolbar, then choose one of the four buttons:

- **911 Alert** — you need assistance now
- **Geo-fence Breached** — a boundary was crossed
- **Ring the Bell** — call attention to your position
- **In Contact** — engaged with the enemy

## Slide to confirm

![Emergency Beacon confirmation with two switches and the OK button](./03-screenshot.png)

Slide both switches to the right, then tap **OK** to initiate the beacon. It keeps broadcasting across the network until you disable it.

## Cancel the alert

![Emergency Beacon window with Cancel Alert chosen from the drop-down](./04-screenshot.png)

Tap the lit **Emergency** button again to reopen the window. Choose **Cancel Alert** from the drop-down, slide both switches to the right and tap **OK**. The beacon clears on every TAK device in the network.
