# Read Your Status & Location

META: slides

Once you are enrolled, the main TAK Tracker window shows your position, connection health and movement data at a glance. It works in portrait or landscape.

## Open the main window

![TAK Tracker main window showing location, connection and data boxes](./01-screenshot.png)

After enrollment the main window fills with live data. Every box updates on its own — tap a box to change what it shows or which units it uses.

## Read your coordinates

![Location box showing latitude and longitude in degrees, minutes and seconds](./02-screenshot.png)

The location box shows your coordinates. The default format is **degrees / minutes / seconds (DMS)** — tap the box to switch it to **MGRS**.

## Check your connection

![Connection box showing Server Connected, GPS Available and Compass High](./03-screenshot.png)

The connection box shows three health indicators:

- **Server** — shows an error if enrollment failed
- **GPS** — shows _not available_ until you enable location services from the Android pull-down drawer
- **Compass** — ideally **High**; _Moderate_ or _Unreliable_ usually means magnetic interference

## Read altitude, speed, compass and heading

![Four boxes showing Altitude, Speed, Compass and Heading](./04-screenshot.png)

The other four boxes show your movement data. Tap any box to change its units:

- **Altitude** — m or ft
- **Speed** — m/s, kps, fps or mph
- **Compass** and **Heading** — °MN or °TN
