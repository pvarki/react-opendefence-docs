# Enroll on a Server

META: slides

Connect TAK Tracker to your TAK Server so your position shows up for everyone else on the mission. Takes about a minute once permissions are granted.

## Open the Enrollment window

![TAK Tracker Enrollment window with Server, Port, Username and Password fields](./01-screenshot.png)

Once the Android permissions are granted, the **Enrollment** window opens. The **Server** field is the URL every user on the mission logs into — everyone must be in the same client enroll group to see each other.

## Enter your details and enroll

Fill in the fields, then tap **ENROLL**:

- **Server** — the server URL
- **Port** — leave the default **8446** unless your server uses a non-standard port
- **Username** and **Password** — your enrollment credentials (the username does not have to match your callsign)

On success a notification appears and the main window fills with your data. Tap **ADVANCED** instead to set your callsign and reach extra enrollment options — this bypasses the simple enrollment screen.
