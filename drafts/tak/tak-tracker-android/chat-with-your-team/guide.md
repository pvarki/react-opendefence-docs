# Chat With Your Team

META: slides

TAK Tracker can send and receive text messages with everyone on the server — ATAK, WinTAK and other TAK Tracker users alike.

## Open Chat and send a message

![TAK Tracker chat window with messages and the on-screen keyboard](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap **Chat** on the toolbar to open the messaging window, type your message and send it. Anyone enrolled on the same server can read it and reply.

## Spot unread messages

The **Chat** icon shows the number of unread messages in red. Tap it to open the conversation and clear the count.
