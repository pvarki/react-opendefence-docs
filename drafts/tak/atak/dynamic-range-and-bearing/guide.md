# Dynamic Range & Bearing

META: slides

A range-and-bearing line you can drop, pin in place, and shove around the map as the picture changes — unlike the fixed two-point measure line.

## Drop the line

![Dynamic R&B Line radial menu with Pin and Lock Line options](./01-screenshot.png)

From the Range Tools toolbar, select the **Dynamic R&B Line** icon. Tap once to place an endpoint, tap again for the second point. The line stays unlocked so you can keep moving it.

## Pin it in place

![Pinned R&B line on the map showing bearing and distance](./02-screenshot.png)

Tap the centre of the line and use the **Pin** button on the radial to fix the position. The pinned line shows azimuth and distance and stays put even after the Dynamic R&B Line is toggled off.

## Reposition or open the endpoint radial

![R&B Line endpoint radial with the Fine Adjust icon](./03-screenshot.png)

To move a pinned line, long press either end and tap a new location. To tune one end precisely, select that end to open the radial, then tap **Fine Adjust**.

## Fine adjust the end

![Magnified crosshair view with Confirm and Cancel buttons](./04-screenshot.png)

Crosshairs appear over a magnified area. Drag a finger inside it to position the end exactly, then select **Confirm** to accept or **Cancel** to discard.
