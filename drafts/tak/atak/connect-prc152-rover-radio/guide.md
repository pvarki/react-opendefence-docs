# Connect to PRC-152 / Rover Radio

META: slides

Wire your phone to a Harris PRC-152 radio and a Rover video downlink so you can talk point-to-point and watch live aircraft video on the map.

## Open Radio Controls

![Radio Controls panel listing Harris SA, Harris Soft KDU and Rover](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Radio** icon to open **Radio Controls**. **Harris SA** is the PRC-152 voice and data radio; **Rover** is the ISRV video downlink.

## Set the PRC-152 cable

![Cable Configuration window with the PRC-152A Cable dropdown selected](./02-screenshot.png)

Connect the PRC-152 cable, then tap the **Harris SA** gear icon. In **Cable Configuration**, pick your cable (e.g. **PRC-152A Cable**) and tap **OK**.

## Connect the PRC-152

![Radio Controls over the map with the Harris SA toggle](./03-screenshot.png)

Slide the **Harris SA** toggle from **OFF** to **ON**. Once connected, other radios on the same net appear on the map when squelched.

## Connect the Rover

![Radio Controls showing Rover toggled ON and Connected ANALOG](./04-screenshot.png)

Slide the **Rover** toggle from **OFF** to **ON**. ATAK scans for a feed; the antenna icon turns green and reads **Connected** when the downlink is live.

## Open Rover Controls

![Rover Controls showing frequency, Scan, Next and Watch buttons](./05-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Rover** menu item to open **Rover Controls**. The current frequency and waveform show at the top. Use **Scan** and **Next** to step through feeds.

## Set frequency and waveform

![Scan Frequency Range menu with Waveform dropdown and band buttons](./06-screenshot.png)

Tap **Scan** to open **Scan Frequency Range**. Enter a **Start Freq Code** and **End Freq Code**, pick the **Waveform** (e.g. ANALOG, DDL), and tap **OK**, then **Next** to advance.

## Watch the video

![Live Rover video downlink with target grid and coordinate overlay](./07-screenshot.png)

Tap **Watch** to open the live stream. The video shows the aircraft sensor feed with target grid and coordinates overlaid.
