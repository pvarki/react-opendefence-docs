# Digital Pointer

META: slides

Point at a spot on the map and everyone on your network sees it live, with range and bearing from you and optional threat rings.

## Drop a live pointer

![Digital Pointer radial menu showing the Object Marker in the centre](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Digital Pointer** icon, then **Pointer** to drop a marker on the map. Anyone on your network sees it appear and gets a notification, with a line running back to you.

## Show range, bearing and threat rings

![Radial menu with Range and Bearing Line and Custom Threat Rings options](./02-screenshot.png)

Tap your own pointer to open its radial menu. Pick **Range and Bearing Line** to show distance and direction from you, or **Custom Threat Rings** to add danger rings around the spot.

## Use a teammate's pointer

![Radial menu for another user's pointer with Lock to Self and Track Breadcrumbs](./03-screenshot.png)

Tap someone else's pointer for extra options:

- **Lock to Self** to keep it centred on your position
- **Bloodhound Tool** to navigate to it
- **Track Breadcrumbs** to follow its trail

## Point at an exact grid

![GoTo MGRS option for entering coordinates by hand](./04-screenshot.png)

Tap **GoTo MGRS** on the Digital Pointer toolbar to type a 10-digit Easting and Northing by hand. The pointer drops on that exact grid for the current map view.
