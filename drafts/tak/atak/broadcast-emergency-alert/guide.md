# Broadcast Emergency Alert

META: slides

Send an emergency beacon to everyone on the network so they see your position and the type of trouble you are in.

## Open the Alert Tool

![ATAK map with the My Emergency Beacons panel open on the right side](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Alert** icon (warning triangle) to open the Alert Tool. It puts an emergency beacon at your location on the map.

## Pick the emergency type

![Drop-down menu listing 911 Alert, Ring The Bell, Geo-fence Breached, In Contact](./02-screenshot.png)

Open the **Select the type of emergency** drop-down and choose one:

- **911 Alert** — need assistance now
- **Ring The Bell** — call attention to your position
- **Geo-fence Breached** — a boundary was crossed
- **In Contact** — engaged with the enemy

## Enable both switches to broadcast

![My Emergency Beacons panel with two LOCKED switches that must both be enabled](./03-screenshot.png)

After picking the type, flip both **LOCKED** switches on. The TAK Server then broadcasts your beacon to every network contact. The beacon keeps broadcasting even if your device is turned off.

## Cancel the beacon

![My Emergency Beacons panel showing the switches that are toggled off to cancel](./04-screenshot.png)

To stand down, return to the **Alert** tool and toggle both switches off. This cancels the beacon and removes it from the map.
