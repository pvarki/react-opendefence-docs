## Hit Send to see sending options

- The **SEND button** in bottom left lets you send your markers.

## Sending options

- You can send markers directly to Feeds, GeoChat Groups, Roles, Teams (people with same marker color than you) and individuals.
- The OpenDefence standard model for TAK recommends to send recon markers such as this **to a role -> HQ** (as shown in the pic) or an Observations feed.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=f0dd0d30-fd0a-4d7d-b6af-042f185026b2)

## Who receives?

- Sending to Feeds disseminate your marker to all feed subscribers.
- Sending to Role sends it all bearers of that role.

The marker in the image, sent to the HQ role, will end up for the Command Post's people to be analyzed.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=15de5371-d62d-4593-b984-31f8720641d4)

## Feeds are the best tool to share markers

- Sending markers to users will clog up their maps quickly.
- The best way is to either add markers directly to a feed...
- ...or as our Standard Model instructs, to first send them to the CP who owns the quality of the RECON feed so everything you read from there is cuated.

## Open the marker radial

- Tap a marker to bring up its radial menu.
- Pick **Details** to edit it, or **Fine Adjust** and **Polar Coordinates** to refine its position.

![Marker radial menu showing Details and Fine Adjust](./aug-01.png)

## Set status and modifiers

- In **Details**, set the **Status** field (for example **Present/Damaged**) to show a status indicator under the marker.
- Tick **Show Modifiers** to record direction of movement, speed and location, then send the update with **Send** or **Auto Send**.

![Marker details pane with Status field and Show Modifiers checkbox](./aug-02.png)

## Edit type and affiliation

- Use the marker icon in the top-left of the pane to change the **Affiliation**.
- Select **Type** to choose a more specific category from the list.

![Type selection dialog with marker categories](./aug-03.png)

## Fine Adjust to move it precisely

- Select **Fine Adjust** on the radial to nudge the marker into exact placement.
- Long-press **Fine Adjust** to open the sub-radial and type an **MGRS** or coordinate location directly.

![Marker radial with Fine Adjust highlighted on the map](./aug-04.png)
