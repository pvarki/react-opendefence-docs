## Compass: North-Up vs Track-Up

![Compass showing the current bearing next to the Self-Marker](./aug-01.png)

The **Compass** sits in the upper-left corner. Single-press it to switch between **North Up** (north stays at the top) and **Track Up** (the map turns with your heading, keeping the Self-Marker facing forward).

## Rotate the map by hand

![Compass long-press menu with the Manual Orientation rotate control](./aug-02.png)

Long-press the **Compass** and select **Manual Orientation**, then drag two fingers on the map to spin it to any bearing you like.

## Lock the map rotation

![Compass menu showing the Manual Orientation lock with a padlock icon](./aug-03.png)

To freeze the map at its current angle, long-press the **Compass** and tap **Manual Orientation** again. The padlock icon confirms the orientation is locked; tap once more to unlock.

## Switch screen orientation

![Map redrawn in portrait after using the Orientation control](./aug-04.png)

Select **Orientation** to swap the screen between landscape and portrait, independent of how you are holding the device. Changing it closes any open drop-downs so they redraw correctly.

## Adjust brightness for day or night

![Brightness slider dimming the map across three levels](./aug-05.png)

Select **Brightness** and use the slider to dim or brighten the map for daylight or low-light use. Only the map dims; markers and drop-downs stay fully visible.
