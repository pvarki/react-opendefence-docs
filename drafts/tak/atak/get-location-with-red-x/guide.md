# Get Location with Red X

META: slides

Read the exact coordinates and elevation of any point on the map by dropping the Red X on it.

## Turn on the Red X tool

![ATAK map with the Red X dropped and the location widget open top-right](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap the **White X** on the toolbar to toggle the Red X tool into a movable state. The icon turns yellow.

## Tap to read a location

![Location widget showing coordinates, elevation and range/bearing for the Red X](./02-screenshot.png)

While the icon is yellow, tap anywhere on the map and the Red X jumps there. The widget in the upper right shows that point's coordinates, elevation and range and bearing.

## Lock or disable it

![Map view with the Red X placed and its info widget](./03-screenshot.png)

Tap the toolbar icon again to lock the Red X to its current spot. Long press the **Red X** icon on the toolbar to disable the tool. Note: the Red X is not saved when ATAK is closed and reopened.

## Open the radial menu for more

![Red X radial menu: Fine Adjust, Range and Bearing Line, Compass Overlay, Pair to Self, Polar Coordinates, Place a Marker](./04-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Red X** on the map to open its radial menu. From here you can:

- **Fine Adjust** the position
- drop a **Range and Bearing Line**
- turn on the **Compass Overlay** or **Polar Coordinates**
- **Pair to Self** or **Place a Marker**

Long press the yellow arrow to reach the sub-radial menus.
