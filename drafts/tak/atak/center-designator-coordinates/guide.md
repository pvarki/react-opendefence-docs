# Get Coordinates with Center Designator

META: slides

Turn on the Center Designator to read the exact coordinates and elevation under the middle of your map.

## Enable Center Designator

![ATAK Other Overlays menu with Center Designator at the top and a coordinate readout in the lower-left of the map](./01-screenshot.png)

Open **Overlay Manager**, then **Other Overlays**. Tap the eye icon next to **Center Designator** to turn it on.

## Read coordinates at map centre

![Map view with the centre crosshair and the Center Designator readout showing position and elevation](./02-screenshot.png)

The readout chip in the lower-left now shows the coordinates and elevation at the map centre. Pan the map to read any point.
