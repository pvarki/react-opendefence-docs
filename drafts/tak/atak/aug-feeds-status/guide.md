## Read the feed status colours

![Feed list showing a feed's coloured sync icon](./01-screenshot.png)

The coloured sync icon by each feed tells you its state at a glance:

- **Green** — downloaded and up to date.
- **Yellow** — connected but behind (e.g. a large file still to pull).
- **Grey** — downloaded, but not checking for updates.
- **Red** — downloaded, but you're offline; reconnect to sync.
