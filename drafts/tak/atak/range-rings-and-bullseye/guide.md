# Range Rings & Bullseye

META: slides

Drop range rings or a bullseye grid on the map to judge distance and sector to a point, then share it with your team.

## Drop a range ring circle

![R&B Circle radial menu with Label, Edit, Fine Adjust, Details and Delete around the ring](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Circle** icon on the toolbar to turn the Circle Tool on (yellow), then tap the map for the centre. Tap the circle's centre to open its radial menu.

## Set radius and ring count

![R&B Circle details panel showing Center, Edge, Radius and Rings fields](./02-screenshot.png)

On the radial, tap **Details**. Set the **Radius**, **Rings** count and unit. The circle redraws as specified and can be sent or broadcast to other users.

## Add a bullseye grid

![Map showing a green bullseye grid and a red bullseye grid over markers](./03-screenshot.png)

The **Bullseye** tool adds a circular grid with bearing lines every 30 degrees. Angles read either **From Center** (red, away) or toward the centre point (green).

## Adjust the bullseye

![Bullseye radial menu with Range Rings, Angle Bearing Units, Fine Adjust and Details](./04-screenshot.png)

Tap the bullseye centre for its radial. Use **Range Rings** to add rings, **Angle Bearing Units** to switch units, and **Fine Adjust** to nudge the centre.

## Set direction and send

![Bullseye details panel with Direction From Center, Degrees and Show Range Rings](./05-screenshot.png)

Tap **Details**. Set **Direction** (From Center or toward it), the angle units and **Show Range Rings**. Tap **Send** to broadcast the bullseye to other users.
