# Clear Content

META: slides

## 1. Clearing Content

- All map items you've created in ATAK or received as data packages are stored in your device.
- Clear content in ENDEX to maintain sanity and avoid accumulating data (opsec).
- To do so, tap the **Trashcan icon** in the main menu.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=9108a3c9-0e36-46c0-9c98-b2646f09d7b4)

## 2. Are You Sure?

- Tap the two selectors to enable clearing.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=d6da0752-89c7-493a-8328-aaf736a3b11e)

 

## 3. Complete!

- Clear Content empties everything and closes the app. Done!

## Lock Both Switches to Wipe

- Swipe both switches to the right until they read **LOCKED**; this arms the red **Clear Now** button.
- Tick **Clear maps & imagery** first if you also want offline maps removed. ATAK exits once the wipe finishes.

![Clear Content dialog with both switches LOCKED and the red Clear Now button active](./aug-01.png)

## Delete Only Some Items

- Instead of wiping everything, tap **Select Items** to open the Overlay Manager multi-select tool and pick exactly what to remove.
- Tap **Cancel** to back out to the map. Deleted file data is corrupted on disk, so recovery is not possible.

![Clear Content dialog highlighting the Select Items button for a granular delete](./aug-02.png)
