# Customize Markers & Icons

META: slides

Pick the right symbol for what you are reporting, switch to mission-specific iconsets, and tune your own Self-Marker so the team can read you at a glance.

## Pick a marker subtype

![ATAK Select Subtype panel listing Ground, Air Defense, Aircraft and other subtypes with a Search option](./01-screenshot.png)

After choosing a marker, tap the subtype field to open **Select Subtype**, then pick the affiliation you need. Use **Search** to jump straight to a subtype instead of scrolling the list.

## Switch to a mission iconset

![ATAK Select Icon Pallet panel listing Markers, Mission, Spot Map, Vehicle Models and Google iconsets](./02-screenshot.png)

Swipe the iconset area or tap the **Iconset Name** field to open the pallet list, then choose **Mission** for mission-specific symbols such as Contact Point (CP), Initial Point (IP) and Observation Point (OP).

## Customise your Self-Marker

![ATAK Self-Marker radial menu with Fine Adjust, Polar Coordinates and User Details options around the blue self arrowhead](./03-screenshot.png)

Set your marker size, main colour and outline under **Settings > Display Preferences > My Location Color/Size**. Use the **Change/Create Icon Main Custom Color** and **Change/Create Icon Outline Custom Color** sliders to make it stand out on the map.

## Place it with Fine Adjust

![ATAK Fine Adjust sub-radial offering MGRS Location, Enter Coordinates and Self Marker placement](./04-screenshot.png)

Long-press **Fine Adjust** on the Self-Marker radial to open the sub-radial. Enter an exact **MGRS Location** or coordinate to drop yourself precisely instead of relying on GPS drift.
