# Capture & Broadcast Drone Video

META: slides

Push your drone feed to the whole team, grab stills, and record video to your device straight from the UAS Tool toolbar.

## Open Drone Utilities

![UAS Tool live feed with the Drone Utilities toolbar down the left edge, labelling Broadcast Video, Mapshot, Drone Shot, Record Video and Platform Settings](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Drone Utilities** wrench icon on the lower toolbar to open the capture tools: Broadcast Video, Map Shot, Drone Shot, Record Video and Platform Settings.

## Broadcast video to the team

![The left toolbar with the Broadcast Video icon highlighted; it turns green once broadcasting is live](./02-screenshot.png)

Tap **Broadcast ON** to stream the feed to other TAK users over Wi-Fi or tactical radio. The button turns green when the stream is live. Teammates open it with **Video** on the UAS map marker radial.

## Set up the broadcast

![The same toolbar, indicating a long press on the Broadcast button opens the broadcast settings](./03-screenshot.png)

Long-press **Broadcast On** to open the broadcast settings. Set the **Broadcast Size**, **Broadcast Bitrate** and **Video Broadcast Destination** to match your network.

## Drop a Mapshot on the map

![The toolbar with the Mapshot icon marked; it captures the current feed and drops it as a map marker](./04-screenshot.png)

Tap **Mapshot** to grab the current feed image and drop it as a picture marker on the map. A prompt lets you send it to the team, and it lands at the spot the drone was looking at.

## Take a Drone Shot still

![The Drone Shot icon, a drone with a camera, used to capture a still from the platform sensor](./05-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Drone Shot** icon to capture a still straight from the platform's sensor and save it to the device.

## Record video locally

![The toolbar with the Record Video icon marked; it starts and stops recording the incoming feed to the device](./06-screenshot.png)

Tap **Record Video** to start and stop recording the incoming feed. Clips save to the EUD and show up in ATAK's Video Player. Long-press the button to record to the EUD, the drone's SD card, or both.
