# Play & Capture Video

META: slides

Watch live IP-camera, Rover5 and H.264 video feeds in ATAK, grab a still, record clips, and push the feed onto the map.

## Open a video and play it

![Video Player list showing stored and streaming video aliases](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Video Player** icon to open the list of feeds. Select a video alias or file name to start playing.

## Use the playback controls

![Video playing in half-screen with play, pause, time and mute controls along the bottom](./02-screenshot.png)

The video shows at half-screen with **play/pause**, elapsed time and **mute/unmute**. Slide the pull bar to go full screen; press the device **Back** button to return.

## Snapshot a frame

![Video Snapshots gallery showing saved JPEG frames as thumbnails](./03-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Snapshot** icon to save the current frame as a JPEG; the icon flashes green when done. Tap the snapshots icon to open the **Video Snapshots** gallery.

## Send a snapshot

![Single snapshot open with Send, Edit and other options below it](./04-screenshot.png)

Open a snapshot, then tap **Send** to push it to another TAK user, the TAK server, or a 3rd party app. Use **Arrow** to cycle to the next snapshot.

## Record the feed to a file

![Auto Record control with the red Record button](./05-screenshot.png)

For a live UDP or RTSP stream, tap **Record** to start; it turns into a green square while recording. Tap **Record** again to stop. Clips save to /atak/tools/videos/.

## Show the feed on the map

![Live video feed overlaid on the map imagery](./06-screenshot.png)

If the stream carries corner metadata, tap the **Globe** icon in the top-right of the video window. The globe turns green and the feed overlays directly on the map.
