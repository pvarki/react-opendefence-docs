# Open UAS Tool & Read Telemetry

META: slides

Find a drone on the network, open its feed, and read its altitude, battery and link without leaving ATAK.

## Open the drone list

![UAS Tool Home Screen showing the Network UAS list with several connected drones](./01-screenshot.png)

The UAS Tool Home Screen lists every drone on the network under **Network UAS**. Drones in **UAS Observer** mode have a black background; an **Operator Connected** drone sits on top with a blue background. Each entry shows signal strength and battery at a glance.

## Select a platform

![A selected drone expanded to show its ribbon menu and Operator details](./02-screenshot.png)

Tap a drone to select it and open its ribbon. The ribbon exposes **Platform Settings**, **Platform Health**, **Platform Information** and **Pan To**. Only one drone can be selected at a time.

## Open the UAS Display

![Full Motion Video feed with map, instrument panel and metadata overlays](./03-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Play** button to open the UAS Full Motion Video (FMV) display. The video fills the screen with the **Quick Flight Toolbar** on top, the **Instrument Panel** (heading, horizon, GPS fix) on the right and the **Lower Toolbar** along the bottom.

## Read the status overlay

![sUAS Metadata overlay showing battery percentage, link bars, range, bearing and altitude](./04-screenshot.png)

The **sUAS Metadata** in the upper-left gives you the live numbers: remaining battery, connection strength, range and bearing from your position, and altitude (**AGL**). Check these before and during every flight.
