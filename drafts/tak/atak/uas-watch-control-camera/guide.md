# Watch & Control Drone Camera

META: slides

Get eyes on the drone's live video, aim the camera, and pull a grid on what you see.

## Open the live video feed

![ATAK UAS Tool split screen with the live FMV video on the right and the map on the left](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap **Play** on the UAS Tool home screen to open the Full Motion Video (FMV) feed and map. The drone's marker, its **SPoI** (Sensor Point of Interest) and camera field of view show on the map.

## Pan and tilt the camera

![Gimbal Control toolbar showing the Pan/Tilt gimbal sliders, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Snap Gimbal](./02-screenshot.png)

Tap **Gimbal Control** to open the gimbal toolbar. Drag the **Pan/Tilt** sliders to aim the camera, and use **Zoom In** / **Zoom Out** to move in or out.

## Lock the gimbal

![Lower toolbar icon strip with the Gimbal Lock button numbered 7](./03-screenshot.png)

Tap **Gimbal Lock** (item 7 on the lower toolbar) to fix the camera's yaw so it stays pointed and does not rotate with the drone.

## Switch thermal or visible

![Select Video Stream dialog with Frontal and Thermal options](./04-screenshot.png)

Open the **Camera** options, tap **Select Video Stream**, and pick **Frontal** (visible) or **Thermal**. Use thermal in low light or to pick out heat.

## Read the crosshair (SPoI)

![FMV crosshair with the grid coordinate, slant range and bearing labelled](./05-screenshot.png)

The center crosshair marks where the camera is looking. It shows **Grid Coordinates**, the **Range** (slant distance to the point) and the **Bearing** (angle from the drone). Use this to call a grid.

## Snap the map to the drone

![FMV view with the Pan-To button highlighted on the lower toolbar](./06-screenshot.png)

Tap **Pan-To** to jump the map view to the drone's location. Long press **Pan-To** to lock the map to the drone marker so it follows along.

## Toggle full or half screen

![FMV view with the screen size button highlighted on the lower toolbar](./07-screenshot.png)

Tap the screen size button to switch between **Half screen** (big map, small video) and **Full screen** (big video with a mini-map). Tap it again to switch back.
