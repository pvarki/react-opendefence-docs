# Resection (No-GPS Location)

META: slides

No GPS signal? Take bearings to two or more known landmarks and ATAK works out where you are.

## Open the Resection Tool

![Empty Resection panel showing Point, Location and Bearing columns with an empty intersection field](./01-screenshot.png)

Open the **Resection Tool**. The panel lists each landmark with its **Location** and **Bearing**, plus an empty **Intersection** field at the bottom.

## Drop a landmark and set its bearing

![Compass dial displayed for sending a bearing to a landmark, with cardinal points around a red needle](./02-screenshot.png)

Stand still, face a landmark you can see on the map, and tap the point dropper icon to place it. A compass appears so you can adjust the bearing from your position to that landmark.

## Plot the intersection

![Map with the Resection panel listing three landmarks and a computed intersection point](./03-screenshot.png)

Place two or more landmarks. Then tap the **Plot Intersection** icon to drop a marker at the computed intersection. The marker is labelled with your device callsign.

## Move, delete, or clear landmarks

![Map view with landmark points placed and the populated Resection panel](./04-screenshot.png)

- To move a point, long-press it on the map and pick a new spot.
- To remove one point, tap it on the map and select **Delete**.
- To remove them all, tap the **Clear Landmarks** icon and confirm.

## Update your position and exit

![Update location dialog asking whether to update your location to the resection estimate](./05-screenshot.png)

Tap **Back** to exit. When asked to update your location, select **Yes** to move your Self-Marker to the estimate and broadcast it, or **No** to leave it.
