# Use 3D Terrain View

META: slides

Tilt the map into 3D to read terrain, slopes and dead ground. You need elevation data installed (DTED, SRTM, or Quantized Mesh).

## Open the 3D menu

![Compass controls strip showing the North Arrow, rotate icon and 3D label](./01-screenshot.png)

Long press the **North Arrow** to open the extra controls, then select **3D**.

## Start from the flat map

![Top-down 2D map view with green and grey overlays before tilting](./02-screenshot.png)

With 3D on, a tilt angle indicator appears around the **North Arrow**. The map still looks flat until you tilt it.

## Two-finger tilt

![Tilted 3D perspective showing terrain elevation with overlays raised above the surface](./03-screenshot.png)

Touch the screen with two fingers and swipe up or down to tilt the view. Items with set elevations rise above the surface. Tilt to 90 degrees to look straight at a marker.

## Lock the 3D view

![Compass controls strip with the 3D control showing a lock icon](./04-screenshot.png)

Once the angle is right, select the **3D Lock** icon. The 3D view stays locked while you pan the map.
