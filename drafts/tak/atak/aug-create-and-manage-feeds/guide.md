# Create & Manage Feeds

META: slides

## Creating Feeds

- Create feeds for either delivering your Plans or to start continuously sharing information.
- Tap the **Feed symbol** to start.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=27f7aa9f-081d-4c4a-9623-09619734107e)

## The Datasync Home - Overlay Manager

- Datasync home shows all the present feeds in the servers you are connected to.
- Hit the **Plus symbol** to create a new Feed.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=c1f63e0e-89f5-47ca-95ca-34c82dbb41a9)

## Creating a Feed

- Tap **Create feed.**

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=0a81e14e-a45c-45b6-ae93-bcb16a1854b5)

## Creating Feed - Options

- First we want to name your new feed.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=88882722-d10b-41fb-8136-858af30d9636)

## Name your Feed

- Be descriptive. The example name points out that the feed contains your plan for the FRAGO3 Order.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=3a1046bc-41f3-4173-b61b-e0f5488b0fdb)

## Choose Default Role

- Next, tap the Default Role selector to choose who can access your Feed.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=11eff933-158b-4d7e-a353-4be9cea0d318)

## Feed Default Access

- Choose what best suites your purposes.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=641fe70a-1536-4ca4-9abe-0fb7f0cb5dee)

## Describe your Feed

- By hitting Description, you may describe your feed a bit. That helps.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=3cbdf11b-6614-4b74-8148-f4a82458d769)

## Hashtags

- Optionally, you could write some hashtags for your feed. That could help in analyzing data on your server.

![](https://pvarki.getoutline.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=c22bb4e2-5298-48fa-95de-0b61e21abe73)

## New Feed Created!

- Your new feed is now here!
- If the default role is not Admin, people from your server can now subscribe your feed.

After successful testing Create & Manage Feeds, continue to [Draw & share Plans](/doc/2676ebbb-83f4-4569-a7a2-7050395f28c8)

## Confirm in the Data Sync List

- Reopen Data Sync. Your new feed now appears in the list with its callsign and item count.
- Tap the **eye icon** beside it to toggle visibility on the map.
