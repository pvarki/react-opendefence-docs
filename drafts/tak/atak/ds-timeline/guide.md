# Feed Timeline View

META: slides

Timeline view shows a feed's items as a scrollable stack of snapshots — handy for catching up on what changed.

## Open Timeline

![The Timeline view with feed tiles](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Timeline** icon from the feed list or a feed dashboard. Switch to **portrait** when prompted. Pick a feed tile, or **All** to see every feed you follow.

## Read and act on snapshots

![Timeline snapshots with map captures](./02-screenshot.png)

Each snapshot shows the **callsign**, time, location and a map capture; tap it to show the item on the map. Tap the **⋮** on a snapshot for **View Details → History** (revert to an earlier version), **Save screenshot**, **Contact Author** or **Undo**.
