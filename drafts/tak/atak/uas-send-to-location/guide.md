# Send Drone to a Location

META: slides

Push the drone to any point on the map, orbit a target, or have it follow you, all from the Quick Flight Toolbar without building a route.

## Open Quick Task

![Quick Task dialog showing Reposition or Task the UAS with a selected coordinate and Cancel, Go Now, Orbit buttons](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap a spot on the map to send the drone there. **Quick Task** opens with the location ready and three options: **Cancel**, **Go Now**, **Orbit**.

## Fly there now

![Quick Task dialog with the Go Now button highlighted at the bottom](./02-screenshot.png)

Tap **Go Now**. The drone takes off if needed and flies to the point, holding its current altitude (**HAL**, Height Above Launch).

## Set an exact coordinate

![Selected Coordinate field in the Quick Task dialog showing a grid reference and altitude](./03-screenshot.png)

For a precise point, tap the **Selected Coordinate** field and type the grid and altitude. This is more exact than tapping the map.

## Orbit a target

![Quick Task dialog with the Orbit button highlighted at the bottom](./04-screenshot.png)

Tap **Orbit** to circle the point and watch it. A window lets you set the orbit radius and speed; then use the sticks to grow or shrink the circle and reverse direction mid-flight.

## Follow Me

![Follow Me button icon: a drone, three chevrons and a standing person](./05-screenshot.png)

Tap **Follow Me** in the UAS Tool. The drone locks onto your ATAK GPS self-marker and keeps the same range and bearing as you move.

## Set the follow distance

![Same Follow Me button icon, long-pressed to open follow options](./06-screenshot.png)

Long press **Follow Me** to open a dialog. There you can pick a different marker to follow and set the stand-off distance.
