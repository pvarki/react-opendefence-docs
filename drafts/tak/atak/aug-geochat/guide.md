## Limitations of Geochat

There are three limitations with Geochat:

- 1. Do not attempt to send images / attachments, this is not usable. Send them to a Feed instead.
- 2. Recipient needs to have their TAK app open or in background to receive you.
- 3. iTAK is unreliable with GeoChat group chat, especially with WinTAK <-> iTAK.
     For these reasons, we recommend to use Matrix (Element) for unit comms with Deploy App.

After successful testing GeoChat, continue to [ATAK](/doc/685f6f0f-e7ca-4ba3-807f-568582663e95)

## Check Read Status

- Each direct message shows a **read** tick: a filled white checkmark means the recipient has opened it, an unfilled one means delivered but not yet read.
- The **Messages** list flags states like "sent to offline user" so you know if your message landed.

![Messages list showing per-message delivery and read status](./aug-01.png)

## Create or Add to a Group

- In **Contacts**, select the **Group** icon to make a new chat group of online and offline users; enter a **group name** and **Select**.
- Pick **Select an Existing Group** to drop more users into a group you already have, even while they are offline.

![Create a New Group dialog with a name field and Select an Existing Group option](./aug-02.png)
