# Downloading Maps for Offline use

META: slides

Download map tiles onto your phone so the map keeps working with no signal.

## Open Maps & Favorites

![Maps & Favorites with the Imagery, Mobile and Favorites tabs](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Maps & Favorites** icon. It has three tabs: **Imagery**, **Mobile**
and **FAVS** (favourites).

## Mark the area to download

![Selecting a download area on the Mobile tab](./02-screenshot.png)

On the **Mobile** tab toggle to **Online**, then tap **Select Area**. Pick
**Rectangle**, **Free Form**, **Lasso** or **Map Select** and draw your region.

## Set the resolution

![The resolution slider showing the tile count](./03-screenshot.png)

Drag the slider ends to set detail — **coarse** for fewer tiles, **fine** for
sharper maps. ATAK shows the tile count; the limit is 850,000 tiles per download.

## Name it and download

![The Download Layer dialog](./04-screenshot.png)

Tap **Download**, name the tileset and tap **OK**. A progress bar appears;
**Cancel** stops it. You can also add tiles to an existing set or save an overlay.

## Switch to your offline maps

![The Local tab listing downloaded map layers](./05-screenshot.png)

Toggle to **Local** to list your downloaded layers, nearest first. Tap one to
use it as the map source — the map now works with no signal.

## Manage and share imagery

![The Imagery tab with send and delete controls](./06-screenshot.png)

The **Imagery** tab lists all your maps. Expand a category to **Send** a layer
to other users or delete it. The **Outline** checkbox shows each layer's border.

## Bookmark a view

![The Favorites tab with saved views](./07-screenshot.png)

On **FAVS**, tap **Add Current View** to save your spot and its imagery. Tap a
saved view to jump back, or **Send** it to share with your unit.
