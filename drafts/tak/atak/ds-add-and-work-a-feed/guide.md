# Add to & Work a Feed

META: slides

Once you own or join a feed, the dashboard is where you add content and keep it in sync.

## Open the feed dashboard

![The feed dashboard with its tool icons and item tiles](./01-screenshot.png)

Tap a feed to open its dashboard. The tools across the top are **Add Items**, **Timeline**, **Download**, **Feed Settings** and **Search**. Items are grouped into **Map Items**, **Photos** and **Files**.

## Add items to a feed

![The Add Items menu](./02-screenshot.png)

Tap **Add Items**, then choose how to add: **Map Select**, **Overlays**, **File Select** or **User Log**. The same menu has **Invite** (add teammates) and **Geofence** — anyone whose marker crosses a feed's geofence is invited automatically.

## Update shared items

![Dashboard with changed items highlighted in red](./03-screenshot.png)

Move or edit a shared item and you're asked to **publish** the change. Changed items show in **red**; tap **Mark Read** to clear them. If two people edit the same item offline, a deconfliction prompt lets you keep the version you want.

## Delete or unsubscribe a feed

![The Delete Feed dialog with two safety switches](./04-screenshot.png)

Tap **Delete** on a feed and choose **Unsubscribe** (stop updates, keep content), **Delete local content**, or **Delete from server** (owners only). Slide **both** switches to the locked position, then confirm.
