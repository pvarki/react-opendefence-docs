# Navigate a Route

META: slides

Follow an existing route to your objective with on-screen distance, heading and turn cues.

## Start navigation on a route

![Route Navigation radial icon, a turn arrow](./01-screenshot.png)

Select the route's navigation radial option, or tap its **Navigation** icon in the Routes listing. Navigation info then appears in the top left corner. (To build a route first, see Create Routes and use VNS.)

## Read the navigation info

![Top-left navigation bar showing distance, speed and Next: CP1 Continue Straight](./02-screenshot.png)

The bar shows distance, current speed and the next check point, for example **Next: CP1 Continue Straight**. Tap the top row of the display to collapse it and free up screen space.

## Cues, mute and units

![Navigation bar with cue, Mute and unit controls](./03-screenshot.png)

As you near a check point its cue shows; turn the volume up to hear voice cues, or tap the **Mute** icon to silence them. Tap the distance or speed to cycle units (mph, km/h, kn, m/s).

## Move between check points and end

![Navigation bar with forward and back arrows and the x close control](./03-screenshot.png)

Use the arrows on either side of the info to step to the next or previous check point. Select the **x** to end navigation.

## Navigate tilted in 3D with billboards

![3D-mode navigation with a marker image billboard popped up](./04-screenshot.png)

While navigating in 3D, a marker's attached image pops up as a billboard when you come within its render distance, then clears once you pass. Adjust it under **Settings > Tool Preferences > Specific Tool Preferences > Route Preferences > Billboard Render Distance**.

## Quick Nav to any point (bloodhound)

![3D navigation view with route and check points on the map](./05-screenshot.png)

Select the **Quick Nav** icon to bloodhound straight to any point, object or route. A pairing line is drawn and the lower left shows time, speed and distance to the next check point as you move.
