# Overlay Manager - How to manage the views

META: slides

Overlay Manager sorts everything on your map into categories so you can clean up the picture, find a marker fast, and control exactly what you see.

## Open Overlay Manager

![Overlay Manager category list showing Team, Markers, Attachments, Shapes and more](./01-screenshot.png)

Select the **Overlay Manager** icon to open the list of categories: Team, Markers, Alerts, Data Packages, Navigation, Shapes, Hashtags and more. Tap a category to drill into its items.

## Show or hide layers

![Markers row with three eye states: open, outlined and crossed out](./02-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Visibility** (eye) button to toggle a layer on or off. A white eye means visible, an outlined eye means some items are hidden, and an eye with a line through it means hidden.

## Filter markers by side and domain

![Marker grid sliced by affiliation rows F N H U and domain columns P A G S U](./03-screenshot.png)

In the Markers category select **Slicer**. Tap **F**, **N** and the others to toggle Friendly, Neutral, Hostile and Unknown; the top letters cover Space, Air, Ground, Sea and Subsurface.

## Show only your own markers

![Toggle Filters dialog with Auto Send and Self Authored options](./04-screenshot.png)

Tap **List**, then **Filters**. Turn on **Self Authored** to show only markers you placed on this device and hide everyone else's, then tap **OK**.

## Search the overlays

![File Overlays list with a search icon in the top toolbar](./05-screenshot.png)

Tap the **Search** icon in the Overlay Manager toolbar to find an overlay by name. Pick a category first to narrow the results.

## Clear temporary items with COP Refresh

![Overlay Manager toolbar with the COP Refresh button highlighted](./06-screenshot.png)

Tap **COP Refresh** to clear temporary items from the map: other users' self-markers, their SPIs and ADSB aircraft tracks. Confirm in the window that appears.

## Tag items with Hashtags

![Hashtags category listing created tags such as hash1 and hash2](./07-screenshot.png)

Open the **Hashtags** category and tap **+** to create a tag. Hashtags group related items so you can search and filter by them later; add the tag and select **Done**.

## Turn on the MGRS grid

![Other Overlays menu with Grid Lines enabled and the MGRS grid drawn over the map](./08-screenshot.png)

In Overlay Manager open **Other Overlays** and toggle **Grid Lines** with its eye icon. The MGRS grid is drawn over the map and adjusts automatically as you zoom.

## Toggle the Heatmap overlay

![Other Overlays menu with Heatmap Overlay enabled, showing colour over the terrain](./09-screenshot.png)

In **Other Overlays**, toggle **Heatmap Overlay** to shade the terrain by elevation. Off-Screen Indicators can be toggled here too, pointing to nearby markers just outside your view.

## Edit overlay colour and thickness

![Line Thickness slider and a red colour swatch for editing a file overlay](./10-screenshot.png)

For file overlays (Shapefiles, KMZ, DRW) tap **Edit** (the pencil icon) in the listing. Drag the **Line Thickness** slider and pick a **Color** to make the overlay easier to read.
