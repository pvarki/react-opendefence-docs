# Scout with First-Person View

META: slides

First-Person View drops your camera to ground level so you can scout terrain, sightlines and cover the way you would actually see them on the ground.

## Open the First-Person tool

![ATAK map in First-Person View with two soft joysticks and the toolbar at the top](./01-screenshot.png)

Open the **First-Person** tool from the **Additional Tools** and **Settings** menu. It places buttons at the top of the map and two soft joysticks on the right and left.

## Enable and pick your viewpoint

![Top-down ATAK map with a banner reading Tap location for First Person View](./02-screenshot.png)

Tap the **First-Person View** button, then tap the spot on the map you want to look from.

## Move and tilt the camera

![First-Person ground view of a building with the right joystick labelled DRIVE and TILT](./03-screenshot.png)

Use the right joystick to scout. It toggles between **DRIVE** to move and **TILT** to angle the camera.

## Adjust your elevation

![First-Person View showing the left joystick used to change elevation](./04-screenshot.png)

Use the left joystick to raise or lower your eye level. Elevation goes above ground level and below ground for a subterranean view.

## Exit First-Person View

![First-Person ground view ready to be closed back to the default map](./05-screenshot.png)

Tap the **First-Person** icon again to leave the perspective and return to the default overhead map view.
