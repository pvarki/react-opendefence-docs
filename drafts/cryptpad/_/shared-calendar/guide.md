# Shared calendar

META: slides

Beyond shared files and collaborative editing, your unit team can keep a shared
calendar. Here is how to create one and share it with the team.

## Shared calendar (1/3)

![Calendars menu](./01-calendar.png)

In addition to files and collaborative editing, your team can use shared
calendars. First, navigate to the **Calendars** menu.

## Shared calendar (2/3)

![Creating and naming a new calendar](./02-calendar.png)

Create a new calendar and name it appropriately. Then select the **Sharing**
menu.

## Shared calendar (3/3)

![Sharing the calendar with the team](./03-calendar.png)

Share the calendar with the team. Your team members will then find this shared
calendar in their own Calendars menu.
