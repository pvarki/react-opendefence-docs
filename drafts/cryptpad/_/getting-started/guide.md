# Getting started with CryptPad

META: slides

CryptPad is your team's secure, end-to-end encrypted collaboration suite. This
guide gets you signed in and creating your first document.

## Welcome to CryptPad

![CryptPad welcome screen](./01-welcome.png)

CryptPad is a secure, end-to-end encrypted collaboration suite. Your team can
create and collaboratively edit documents and share files securely. This Deploy
App server runs your team's own, decentralized CryptPad service.

## Why CryptPad is secure

![Diagram of CryptPad's end-to-end encryption](./02-secure.png)

CryptPad ensures that not even the server administrator can read your files. You
decide who to share with — other users on this Deploy App server also need an
invitation from you to read your files.

## Sign in with Deploy App

![CryptPad sign-in screen](./03-signin.png)

CryptPad is integrated with Deploy App's certificate-based authentication. Click
**Log in to CryptPad** and you are authenticated automatically using your Deploy
App credentials — no separate account or password is needed.

## Your CryptPad dashboard

![CryptPad dashboard](./04-dashboard.png)

After signing in you see your CryptPad dashboard. From here you can create new
documents, access recent files, manage your CryptDrive, and collaborate with
team members.

## Create your first document

![Creating a new document from the dashboard](./05-create.png)

Click the **+** button on your dashboard to create a new document. Choose from
rich text, code, spreadsheets, kanban boards, presentations, whiteboards, forms,
and more. All documents are encrypted automatically.

## Share with your team

![Sharing a document](./06-share.png)

To share a document, click the share button in the toolbar. Share with specific
team members or create a link. Multiple users can edit simultaneously and see
each other's changes in real time.
