# Kanban boards

META: slides

CryptPad's Kanban app is like a board of sticky notes. Arrange tasks by
completion stage, or maintain a situation board for your unit.

## Create a Kanban board

![Creating a Kanban board](./01-step.png)

Click **+** and select **Kanban**.

## Edit the board

![Editing columns and cards on a Kanban board](./02-step.png)

Create columns for workflow stages and add cards for tasks. Shown here is an
example task-completion tracking board.

## Manage task cards

![A task card with details](./03-step.png)

Click a card to add details. You can also add images to cards. Drag and drop
cards between columns to update their status.

## Situation board

![Kanban board used as a situation board](./04-step.png)

The Kanban app can also be used as a situation board. Track units' mission
progress, tasks, and the enemy situation in cards, and share your surface
picture with the command centre.
