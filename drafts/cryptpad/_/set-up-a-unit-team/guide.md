# Set up a unit team

META: slides

This CryptPad instance is unique — it exists only on this Deploy App server. All
data stays on your own infrastructure and disappears completely when the server
is shut down. The following is a simple, effective way to use CryptPad in your
unit: as the person responsible for the command system, start by creating a
top-level team.

## Create a team for your unit (1/8)

![Teams menu in the top-right corner](./01-setup.png)

Create a team for your unit in CryptPad. This gives you a shared drive and
calendar easily. Start by finding the **Teams** menu in the top-right corner.

## Create a team for your unit (2/8)

![Teams menu listing existing teams](./02-setup.png)

The Teams menu shows the CryptPad teams you belong to. Create a new one.

## Create a team for your unit (3/8)

![Naming the new team](./03-setup.png)

Give your team a descriptive name and continue.

## Create a team for your unit (4/8)

![New team visible in the teams menu](./04-setup.png)

Your team is created and visible in the teams menu. Continue by clicking your
team's icon.

## Create a team for your unit (5/8)

![Team shared drive with the Members menu](./05-setup.png)

Here you'll find your team's shared drive. Next, navigate to the **Members** menu
to create an invitation to your team.

## Create a team for your unit (6/8)

![Creating an invite link with recommended settings](./06-setup.png)

To let others join your team, create an invite link. The image shows our
recommended settings. Press **Create link** to generate a shareable invite link.

## Create a team for your unit (7/8)

![Copying the invite link to share](./07-setup.png)

Copy the link and share it via your preferred messaging service. The recipient
must be on your unit's Deploy App server — without Deploy App's mTLS certificate,
your CryptPad cannot be accessed, for security reasons.

## Create a team for your unit (8/8)

This is what the joining member's view looks like. After this, they will see
your team's shared drive.
