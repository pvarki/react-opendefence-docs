# Back up your data

META: slides

When a mission ends, back up your team's shared CryptDrive so you can carry it to
your next Deploy App server — or retire it safely.

## Back up your data

![Team Settings menu with the backup option](./01-backup.png)

From the team's **Settings** menu you can easily back up the team's shared
CryptDrive. When your mission ends, take a backup — you can upload it to your
next Deploy App CryptPad server or another system as needed. Remember safe data
handling!
